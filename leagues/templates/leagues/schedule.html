What are the goalie chips?

Each game shows both teams’ goalie status. Tap a chip to open that game on the Goalie Status Board, where anyone — captains, goalies, teammates — can answer Yes, confirmed or No, need a sub for their goalie, or put a sub they lined up in goal with Select a goalie, all in a few seconds with no login. The sooner {{ czar_name }} knows a sub is needed, the easier it is to find one.
